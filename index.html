FAQ

Common Questions, Straight Answers

What are your minimum order quantities (MOQs)?

MOQs vary by category and by factory. The ranges on our category cards — from 1+ for off-road motorcycles to 500-3000 for drinkware — are typical factory minimums. Your exact MOQ is confirmed on your quote.

How long does the sourcing process take?

Our average sourcing cycle is 7 days. We shortlist 3-5 best-fit factories within 48 hours of your request. Sample and production lead times depend on the category and are confirmed in your quote.

What does Xiangda charge buyers?

Nothing. Our sourcing service is completely free for buyers — no upfront fees, no commissions, no markups.

Which shipping terms and destinations do you support?

FOB, CIF and DDP terms are available, with sea, air and express options from Ningbo port. Orders from multiple factories can be consolidated into a single shipment. We currently serve buyers in 30+ countries.
