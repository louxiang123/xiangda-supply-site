FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Key information regarding hardware sourcing and quality control in Yongkang.

Why is Yongkang known as China's Hardware Capital?

Yongkang produces over 70% of China's stainless steel cookware and hardware tools. With thousands of specialized suppliers, raw material casting, metal stamping, and coating are concentrated in one dense cluster.

What are typical MOQs for cookware and hardware in Yongkang?

Standard factory minimums range from 500 to 1,000 sets for custom cookware with custom coating colors or gift box branding. Hand tools with laser logo engraving start around 300 to 500 pieces.

Do Yongkang cookware factories comply with FDA and LFGB food contact standards?

Yes. Export cookware manufacturers in Yongkang supply major global retail chains with PTFE/ceramic non-stick coatings tested and certified under FDA (US), LFGB (Germany), and DGCCRF (France) regulations.

How does quality inspection work for metal hardware?

Xiangda conducts on-site AQL 2.5 inspections verifying material thickness, non-stick coating adhesion (cross-hatch test), salt spray corrosion resistance, and load-bearing strength before export.
