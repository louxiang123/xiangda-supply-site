FAQ

Buyer questions

What is the minimum order for custom thermos flasks?

Typical factory minimums in this category run from 500 to 3,000 pieces, depending on the model and the decoration. The MOQ for your specification is confirmed on the quotation before production.

Can our logo be added, and in what form?

Custom branding is standard in this category. The method depends on the model and the body material, so it is specified on your quotation rather than assumed here.

How much does a custom thermos cost?

There is no published list price, because the unit price moves with body size, insulation type, branding method and order quantity. Send the specification and quantity, and the price comes back with the factory shortlist.

Who holds the test reports and certificates?

Certificates and test reports belong to the factory that produces your order. We verify the document against your model and send you the copy before production starts.
