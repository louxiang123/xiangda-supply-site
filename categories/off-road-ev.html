Price scope

What the price includes — and what it does not

An FOB number and a landed-cost number are different things. Comparing them without this split is how a good quote turns into a bad surprise.

Included

The factory price for the quantity tier, per unit, FOB Ningbo. If you buy across several factories, orders are consolidated into one shipment.

Not included

Sea freight, destination charges, duties and local homologation. Budget those on top before you compare our number with a landed-cost quote.

Quality

FAQ

Buyer questions

What is the MOQ for an electric dirt bike?

It depends on the model. The Mimbob EM22-6 72V and the Nexvey Flash Pro 60V both start at 1 unit; the Mimbob TK5 48V starts at 80 units. MOQ is confirmed on the quotation for your specification.

Is the price per unit or per container?

Per unit, FOB Ningbo. The three published prices are factory FOB for the quantity tier shown on this page. Sea freight, destination charges, duties and local homologation are not included.

Can I put my own logo and colours on the bike?

Yes, all three models are listed as OEM. The Flash Pro 60V states custom logo support in its listing. Colour, decals and packaging changes are confirmed per order, because each change can move the unit price.

Do the bikes come with CE, RoHS or DOT certificates?

Units ship RoHS compliant according to the product listing. The certificate itself belongs to the factory, not to us; we verify the document for your order and send you the copy before production.

How are the bikes inspected before shipment?

Pre-shipment inspection at AQL 2.5 with a photo report, done before the container is booked. If the lot fails, it does not ship.

Do you charge buyers a fee?

No. Buyers pay the factory price; our revenue is a commission from the factory after the order is placed.
