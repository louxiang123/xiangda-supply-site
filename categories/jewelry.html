FAQ

Buyer questions

What is the MOQ for custom jewelry?

Typical factory minimums here run from 10 to 500 pieces per design, depending on the material and how many variants you need. The MOQ for your design is confirmed on the quotation.

Can a factory work from my own design or a photo?

Send a reference image, a sketch or a technical drawing. Whether a specific material or construction is feasible is confirmed per design on the quotation, before any sample is made.

Does the order include packaging?

Packaging is part of this category: pouches, cards and boxes can be specified together with the goods. The exact options are listed on your quotation.

How is quality checked before shipping?

Every order goes through AQL 2.5 pre-shipment inspection with a photo report, issued before the container is booked. Certificates for the materials belong to the factory and a copy is sent to you.
