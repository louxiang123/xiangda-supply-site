Frequently Asked Questions

Key facts regarding toy manufacturing, compliance, and orders in Yiwu.

What are typical MOQs for custom toys from Yiwu?

Factory minimums in Yiwu generally start at 200 to 500 pieces for standard custom packaging or logo printing. For custom injection molds or bespoke plush designs, typical production runs start at 1,000 to 2,000 pieces.

Are Yiwu toy factories compliant with US and EU safety standards?

Yes. Export-oriented toy manufacturers in Yiwu work with accredited testing labs (SGS, TÜV, Intertek) to provide Children's Product Certificate (CPC), ASTM F963, CE, and EN71 safety reports.

Can we inspect the production before shipment?

Yes. Xiangda Supply performs on-site AQL 2.5 pre-shipment inspections covering physical safety, drop tests, small parts hazards, and barcode labeling before container loading.

How does pricing work for toy sourcing?

Toy prices vary based on materials (ABS plastic, plush fabric, FSC wood), compliance testing, and order volume. We deliver a comparison of 3-5 audited factory quotes within 48 hours, 100% free for buyers.
