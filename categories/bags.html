FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Key information regarding bag sourcing, customization, and quality control in Yiwu.

What are typical MOQs for custom bags from Yiwu?

Factory minimums in Yiwu start around 300 to 500 pieces for custom canvas totes, drawstring bags, and cosmetic pouches with custom silkscreen or embroidery branding. Technical backpacks or hard-shell luggage typically start at 500 to 1,000 units.

What branding and logo techniques are available for custom bags?

Options include silkscreen printing, heat transfer, digital DTG, 3D embroidery, woven labels, embossed PU leather patches, and custom metal zipper pullers.

Are eco-friendly and recycled fabrics available in Yiwu?

Yes. Yiwu fabric mills supply GRS-certified RPET (recycled polyester from plastic bottles), OEKO-TEX certified organic cotton canvas, and biodegradable TPU/jute fabrics.

How does quality inspection work for luggage and bags?

Xiangda conducts AQL 2.5 inspections testing zipper cycle endurance, strap pull tension, seam bursting strength, and fabric colorfastness before container loading.
