2. Metallurgy: 304 (18/8) vs 316 (18/10) Stainless Steel

Selecting the right stainless steel alloy directly determines unit cost, thermal corrosion resistance, and consumer satisfaction:

Alloy Grade Chemical Composition Corrosion Resistance Ideal Product Applications Cost Index
SUS 304 (18/8) 18% Cr, 8% Ni Excellent for water, tea, and ice. Resistant to atmospheric oxidation. Standard sports water bottles, outdoor camping flasks, promotional drinkware. Baseline (1.0x)
SUS 316 (18/10) 16-18% Cr, 10-14% Ni, 2-3% Mo Superior acid and chloride resistance due to Molybdenum addition. Zero metallic taste. High-end insulated coffee mugs, milk & juice tumblers, infant thermoses. +15% to +22%
SUS 201 (Outer Only) 16% Cr, 1% Ni, 7% Mn Low corrosion resistance; unsuitable for food contact. Economical outer shell layer only (when paired with a 304 inner liner). -8% to -12%

Factory Configuration Tip: For most Western retail markets, the gold standard is Double-Wall SUS 304 (Inner 304 / Outer 304) or SUS 316 Inner / SUS 304 Outer. We strongly advise international buyers against using SUS 201 outer shells for coastal or dishwasher-safe products, as pinhole rust can develop around the base rim after extended use.

3. Vacuum Insulation Engineering & Thermal Retention

A high-performance vacuum flask depends on three layers of thermal defense:

  1. Double-Wall Vacuum Gap: Air is extracted between inner and outer shells at high temperature, eliminating conductive and convective heat transfer.
  2. Internal Copper Coating (Copper Plating): A micro-thin layer of copper is electroplated onto the outer surface of the inner liner. Copper acts as an infrared heat mirror, reflecting radiant heat back into the liquid.
  3. Sealed Insulated Cap: Multi-threaded PP stopper with food-grade silicone O-rings prevents convective heat escape through the neck.

Standard Temperature Retention Benchmark: Starting with boiling water at 95°C (203°F) at 20°C room temperature, a quality Yongkang flask maintains ≥65°C (149°F) after 12 hours and ≥45°C (113°F) after 24 hours. For cold beverages, iced water at 0°C stays under 10°C (50°F) for up to 24 hours.

6. AQL 2.5 Quality Control & Testing Checklist for Drinkware

Before any container is sealed for Ningbo port dispatch, our on-site inspection team in Yongkang enforces the following test protocol:

  1. Thermal Insulation Vacuum Test: 100% of finished bottles are passed through automated infrared temperature sensors. Any bottle showing exterior wall heating (indicating vacuum seal loss) is automatically rejected.
  2. Inversion & Leakage Test: Filled with boiling water, inverted 180° for 30 minutes, and shaken vigorously. Zero droplet leakage permitted past the silicone seal.
  3. Cross-Hatch Adhesion Test (ASTM D3359): A grid of 1mm lattice cuts is sliced into the painted/powder-coated surface, adhesive 3M tape is applied and ripped at 90°. No paint flaking permitted (Rating 4B/5B).
  4. Drop Impact Test: Dropped from 1.2 meters onto smooth concrete at multiple angles to verify base weld integrity and inner liner stability.
  5. Food Contact Lab Verification: Verification of FDA 21 CFR 175.300 / LFGB non-heavy metal migration certificates (Lead < 0.01 mg/L, Cadmium < 0.002 mg/L, BPA non-detectable).
